Pathway · NCLC 7 commitment

A language objective with verifiable stages

A B1 projection, a B2 assessment and a four-month consolidation cycle define when classes may continue at no additional cost.

01

The objective

Pathway supports progress toward NCLC 7 in listening, reading, speaking and writing for TCF Canada. Learning decisions are based on the student’s results.

02

B1 projection

At B1, we assess the gap between the student’s results and NCLC 7 in each skill, then identify what should be strengthened during B2.

03

Assessment at the end of B2

At the end of B2, all four skills, activities and practice exams are reviewed. This assessment defines the next adjustments; it does not immediately activate classes at no additional cost.

04

Four-month consolidation cycle

A four-month verification and consolidation cycle follows B2. It is billed normally at the pace set out in the study plan. It stabilizes learning, transfers it to the TCF format, checks consistency and applies the adjustments defined in the assessment.

05

Continuation at no additional cost

After this cycle, if NCLC 7 has still not been reached in all four skills and the agreed learning conditions have been met, Pathway continues the language preparation at no additional cost until the language objective is reached.

06

Conditions and scope

The agreed pathway and duration, attendance, participation, tasks and assessments must be completed. The commitment concerns language support; it does not guarantee immigration, residence or a decision by Canadian authorities. Exam and third-party fees are excluded.
