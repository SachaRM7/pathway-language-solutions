The NCLC 7 commitment, documented

Cycles, starting level, attendance and practice exams are confirmed in writing before enrolment.

01

Scope

The pathway supports language preparation and progress toward the skills expected for NCLC 7. It is not immigration advice.

02

Starting level

The Pathway test provides the first estimate; a live class confirms observed skills before the cycle is defined.

03

Follow-up

One cycle corresponds to the planned work for mastering one CEFR level, such as A1 or A2. Attendance, tasks and practice exams are part of the agreed pathway.

04

Exclusions

Exam fees, administrative steps, travel and any IRCC decision remain outside the language service.
