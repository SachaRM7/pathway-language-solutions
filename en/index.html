FAQ

Questions before you start

How long should I plan for?

The calendar depends on starting level, rhythm and exam date. We give a scenario after the test, never an automatic promise.

Does the test measure speaking?

Reading, listening and grammar/vocabulary receive /100. Speaking is not auto-evaluated and is confirmed live.

Can I start month to month?

Yes. Direct cycle payment communicates a 15% reduction; starting with one month and switching to a cycle communicates 10%.
