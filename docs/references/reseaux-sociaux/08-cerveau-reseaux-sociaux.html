Hundreds of things about every video... and Claude reads all of it before it suggests a single idea.

Teach it who you are (10 minutes, once)

Half of what makes Claude sound like AI is that it has ZERO context on you.

Ask it for a caption with zero context and it hands you the average of everyone on the internet... flat, no soul in it.

So you do one interview, out loud, in simple caveman ENGLISH, like you're explaining the business to a friend at the bar.

Claude asks ONE question per message, writes your files in your own words, then saves a Virlo skill so every future chat starts already knowing you:

javascript
Interview me so you can build my social media brain.

Ask me ONE question per message and wait for my answer before the next. Cover:
1. What I sell, who I sell to, and what I'm pushing this month
2. Who buys: what they'd type into TikTok or Instagram search, and the 5-10 brands and creators they already follow
3. My 3-5 biggest competitors (handles if I know them)
4. How I sound: ask me to paste 10 things I've written, then ask which words I'd never use
5. My offer: the one thing I want a viewer to do after watching
6. My platforms and my handles

Keep going until you're 95% sure your writing would pass as mine.
Then save four files: me/about.md, me/audience.md, me/voice.md, me/offer.md.
Then, using Virlo, run build_virlo_skill with everything you learned so every new chat already knows my niche, my competitors and my voice.
Use my words, not yours. If I didn't say it, leave it out.

The rule it never breaks: if you didn't say it, it doesn't go in.

That one line stops Claude inventing a "brand value" you've never heard of.

Here's the voice file it wrote for the habit app:

javascript
# voice.md

How we sound: a friend who's quit a few habits too and came back anyway.

Words we use: streak, day 1 again, small wins, showing up

Sounds like us:

For the ten things you've written, paste what you wrote FAST: replies, captions, the 11pm texts to your cofounder.

Workflow 1: Find the outliers in your niche

This is the one everybody opens with... and the one the people who stick with it never stop running.

You're hunting outliers: videos that did WAY better than that creator usually does.

When an account nobody follows suddenly reaches half the internet, nothing about the account changed overnight... the IDEA did the work, and an idea is something you can borrow.

javascript
Using Virlo, show me the outlier videos in my niche from the last 30 days.

My niche, buyers and competitors are in my Virlo skill.
Only outliers from creators under 100K followers.
TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts. Rank them by how big an outlier each one is, never by raw views.
Leave out paid posts and AI-made videos.

For each one give me: the link, the first line spoken, the on-screen text, how it was shot, and one sentence on why it fits MY buyers.
Then tell me the one pattern that shows up in 3 or more of them.

For the habit app, the top of that list is a kid with a few thousand followers who put ONE line of text on screen:

"how ppl who you have a 100+ streak with look at you in person."

Bro LITERALLY went further with one line of text than most app accounts go all year.

Right under it: a guy "baby proofing" his phone so he doesn't ruin his life when he drinks... from an account so small it wouldn't fill a school assembly.

(That's an APP getting free marketing from a user's joke. Your app could be next.)

Now narrow it to videos you could actually make:

javascript
Same search, but only videos I could film alone on my phone this week: faceless, hands only, or me talking to the camera.
Real filmed footage only.

And find the small creators worth studying (or paying to post for you):

javascript
Using Virlo, find the small creators breaking out in my niche right now.
Under 50K followers, with at least 3 outlier videos in the last 60 days.
Give me a table: handle, followers, their usual views, their best video, and how many outliers they've had.

Workflow 2: Tear apart any competitor

The most common chain people run in Claude is this exact one: pick an account, pull its latest posts, find what popped.

Except now Claude does the scrolling... and it checks each post against that account's OWN average, so a big account's normal Tuesday doesn't fool you.

javascript
Using Virlo, pull @[competitor]'s last 30 videos.

Rank them against their own average views and chart it.
Tell me which 3 are their biggest outliers, and for each one: the first line, the format, how long it is, and what's different about it compared to their flops.

Then get the words.

The script is where the GOLD is, and nobody has time to type out somebody else's video... so Claude does it:

javascript
Get me the full transcripts of those 3 videos and the top 100 comments on each.

Bold the first line of every transcript.
Group the comments into: questions people asked, things they loved, and things they complained about.
Quote every comment exactly as written, then give me 5 video ideas, each built on a real comment.

The comments under your competitor's best video are your audience writing your next script for you.

(A comment like "does it guilt-trip you when you miss a day?" is a question... AND a 30-second video.)

And if they're spending money, see what they're spending it on:

javascript
Using Virlo, find every Meta ad [competitor brand] is running right now.
Sort by how long each has been running, because an ad that's still live after weeks is an ad that's making money.
Then find organic videos in my niche that look like their top 3 ads.

Workflow 3: Steal the hooks, formats and sounds that are winning

Hooks are the number one thing people ask for.

The problem is Claude has never SEEN one, so it writes you "Ever wondered why…" like it's 2019.

Now it can pull real opening lines off real winning videos:

javascript
Using Virlo, what hooks are winning in my niche in the last 14 days?

Give me the top 10 as a table: the exact opening line, what's on screen in the first second, the hook type, views, and a link to the video.
Then tell me which hook TYPE is winning most right now.

Then the formats... the thing that gets copied when nobody copies the words:

javascript
Using Virlo, which formats are creators copying right now that [what you sell] could film this week?

For each format: what stays the same, what I swap in, whether it's brand new, rising or already peaking, and 3 example videos.
Only show me formats that are still rising.

Rising is the only stage worth filming. Peaking means you're late, and late is the most expensive place to be on social media.

Then the sound:

javascript
Using Virlo, any sounds breaking out in my niche this week that most accounts haven't jumped on yet?

Workflow 4: Make it watch your niche while you sleep

Everything above is you asking.

This is the agentic part... where it starts working without you.

You give Claude one goal, and it sets up an agent that goes out every day, collects fresh videos across TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts, and keeps a report waiting for you:

javascript
Using Virlo, set up an agent that watches my niche every day.

Goal: find the videos my buyers can't stop watching (from my Virlo skill).
Search my competitors by name, the problems my buyers have, and people searching for [what I sell].
Turn on hook and format analysis, and keep autopilot on so it swaps out search words that stop working.

Every Monday after that, you ask one thing:

javascript
Using Virlo, what did my agent find this week?

Show me the top 8 videos, what's working (formats, hook styles, who's on camera), any creator breaking out, and any search words autopilot changed and why.

Check that log after a month and you'll find search words you never wrote, because the niche moved and the agent moved with it.

(Turns out, it ALLOWS you to be lazy.)

Workflow 5: Turn it into videos you can film this week

Claude writes the videos now... in your voice, built on videos that already won, with the source video attached.

javascript
Using Virlo, from everything you've found this week, give me 5 hooks for [what you're pushing this month].

Keep each winning hook's structure and swap in my story.
Show each hook with the real video it's based on, its views, and why it works.
Use my voice from me/voice.md and none of my never-use words.

Pick the one you like, then:

javascript
Write a 20-30 second script for hook #2 in my voice.
Give me what I say, the on-screen text and the shot, beat by beat, plus a caption.
Cite the video it came from. Then type "next" until I have a week.

You didn't write a single word of the first draft... and it still sounds like you.

Want it somewhere your team can see? One more line:

javascript
Put this week's videos, hooks and scripts on one page I can share, with every source video playable.

Every Friday, close the loop:

javascript
Using Virlo, track my account (handle in me/about.md). For each video I posted this week, write to memory/findings.md: what it was based on, how it did against my normal, and one thing to change.
When the same lesson shows up 3 times, suggest it as a rule. I decide.

Every flop becomes a rule, and every win becomes a format.

Your week, in 5 prompts

Once it's set up, your week is five prompts:

  1. Monday: "What did my agent find this week?" (Workflow 4)
  2. Monday: the hooks and the rising formats (Workflow 3)
  3. Midweek: tear down whoever just popped off (Workflow 2)
  4. Before you film: 5 hooks, then the scripts (Workflow 5)
  5. Friday: the findings check (Workflow 5)

Research plus planning for the whole week comes in well under an hour... and your only job is the story, the taste and the final yes.
