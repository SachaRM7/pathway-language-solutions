The Pathway method

You always know
what comes next.

One path connects your level, availability, lessons, progress and exam.
No endless modules or unclear priorities.

  1. 01

    Assess

    We assess comprehension and expression, spoken and written, along with the time you have available.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We build your calendar backwards from your exam date.

  3. 03

    Practice

    You work in private lessons and continue between sessions with focused tasks.

  4. 04

    Demonstrate

    Two mock exams check whether all four skills are ready for the TEF or TCF.
